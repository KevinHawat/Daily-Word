Historical and theological context

The Old Testament records Israel’s covenant history, worship, wisdom, and prophetic hope. Catholic reading respects the original literary and historical setting while also reading Scripture in the unity of the whole Bible (CCC 112–114).

Typology understands events and figures of the Old Covenant as anticipating their fulfillment in Christ, without denying their significance in Israel’s own history (CCC 128–130).

Different kinds of links: an explicit quotation is not the same as a thematic parallel or a traditional theological interpretation. The explanations identify these differences.
